Catalyst evidence

    Pre-event neglect

    Analyst coverage, news silence and institutional ownership are unknown. A distressed stock can also score highly on these proxies.

    Scan rules & rating rubric

    Indian-market technical adaptation

    Opening gap = (open / previous close − 1) × 100, strictly >3%. RVOL = today's shares volume / mean shares volume of the previous 20 sessions, strictly >3× (300% of average, not +300%). Close position = (close − low) / (high − low) × 100, ≥65%, meaning the upper 35% of the day's high–low range. Require a positive close-to-close return and close ≥ open. The 3% threshold is a design choice, not a validated optimal level or Stockbee's universal rule.

    General neglect: an explicit proxy

    Use only bars before the event. Prior 20-session return ≤5% earns 30 points, ≤10% earns 20. Prior 60-session return ≤10% earns 25, ≤20% earns 15. Prior 20-day mean volume / preceding 40-day mean volume ≤0.7 earns 25, ≤1.0 earns 15. No earlier +10% day on >3× prior-20 volume in the last 60 sessions earns 20. Strict EP requires score ≥60 and prior-20 return ≤10%. Stocks lacking sufficient history remain unknown, not automatically neglected. Low analyst coverage, fund ownership and general media neglect are not measured.

    Biggest movers versus strict EP

    Biggest movers includes every scanned stock gaining ≥4%, sorted by daily return. Strict EP requires all technical gates and the neglect filter. Volume-led pivots pass volume, close and neglect but lack a >3% gap; they are not strict gap EPs. None of these price buckets alone establishes a catalyst. Full universe lets you inspect all successfully scanned candidates.

    Valid Theme / Story EP

    A separate narrative category, not a loose sector label. Require either a reviewed primary filing with corroborating dated reporting and verified company exposure, or a company-matched positive primary theme disclosure plus at least two reputable media publishers discussing that same theme. Headline-only narratives remain unverified and are excluded. Price confirmation requires a ≥4% up-day, >3× RVOL, close position ≥65%, held open and neglect passed; an opening gap is optional in this separate bucket. Reviewed stories expire after 30 days, and cancellation/withdrawal flags suppress validation. “Valid” means the evidence/exposure criteria pass, not that the thesis is profitable, economically material, or causally proven. Automated feed corroboration is weaker than a full-document review; the detail panel identifies which standard was met.

    Catalyst strength is an evidence score

    1/5: low economic significance, such as corporate-action context. 2/5: a tentative, company-matched bullish event in a media headline. 3/5: positive event language in an exchange feed excerpt, or a primary event reported during the session. 4/5: a specific positive primary disclosure dated before the open, still provisional on business materiality. 5/5 is reserved for independently reviewed exceptional, recurring, materially significant change; this automatic engine never assigns it. Media headlines are capped at 2/5; no claim of an earnings beat, recurring growth or order materiality is made without verification. Routine notices, board-meeting intimations, exchange volume queries and negative headlines do not establish an upward economic catalyst.

    Evidence timing and limitations

    News is collected for every ≥4% mover and every technical gap/volume/close candidate. Eligible evidence runs from the previous trading session's 15:30 IST through the event session's 15:30 IST. Post-open reports may describe a move rather than cause it; news after the close or on later dates is excluded. NSE/BSE feed excerpts and dated Google News headlines are archived with links. Unverified means no qualifying event was found in these fetched sources, not proof that none exists. Sources can be unavailable or incomplete; check Data health.

    Universe, prices and risk

    The source is your existing public Price Cycle dashboard's validated daily universe and adjusted OHLCV chart artifact, not a new exhaustive NSE-wide market-cap census. The inherited range filter is mean(True Range / previous close ×100), not gap-excluding ADR. Displayed close is the raw upstream close; gap and candle calculations use its adjusted OHLCV artifact. Corporate actions, extreme RVOL, distress, zero-range bars and circuit-like moves require checking. RS is inherited from Nifty MidSmallcap 400. Historical charts use the latest available adjusted bars filtered to the selected date, while saved snapshot metrics and news are point-in-time.
